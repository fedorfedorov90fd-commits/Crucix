=== YIELD CURVE ===
URL: /yield-curve
Module: Yield Curve
Category: Financial Monitoring
Icon: 📉

DESCRIPTION:
US Treasury yield curve.
Inversion is a classic recession indicator.

FUNCTIONALITY:
- Current curve shape (normal/inverted)
- 10Y-2Y spread
- Historical spread chart
- Recession risk color coding:
  🔴 Spread < 0 — INVERSION (recession)
  🟡 Spread 0-0.5 — WARNING
  🟢 Spread > 0.5 — NORMAL

DATA SOURCES:
- FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data) — free
