Module: VIX — Volatility Index
Category: Financial Monitoring
Icon: 📈

DESCRIPTION:
VIX (CBOE Volatility Index) — market "fear index".
Shows expected market volatility for the next 30 days.

FUNCTIONALITY:
- Current VIX value
- Fear level color coding:
  🔴 > 30 — HIGH FEAR (crisis)
  🟡 20-30 — MEDIUM FEAR
  🟢 < 20 — NORMAL (calm)
- 30-day historical chart
- Connection with geopolitical events

DATA SOURCES:
- Yahoo Finance (free)
