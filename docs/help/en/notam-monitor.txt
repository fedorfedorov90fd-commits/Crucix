=== NOTAM MONITOR ===
URL: /notam-monitor
Module: NOTAM — Notice to Airmen
Category: Military Monitoring
Icon: 🛩️

DESCRIPTION:
Monitoring of airspace closure notices (NOTAM).
Early indicator of military operations and conflict escalation.

FUNCTIONALITY:
- Display active NOTAM on world map
- Color coding by danger level:
  🔴 CRITICAL (military zone, closure > 24h)
  🟡 HIGH (exercises, closure < 24h)
  🟢 NORMAL (technical work)
- Timeline of closures by region
- Escalation forecast based on NOTAM

DATA SOURCES:
- ICAO (free parsing)
- Eurocontrol (RSS)
- FAA (free)

COMMANDS:
- Click on zone → NOTAM details
- Filter by region
- Export data
