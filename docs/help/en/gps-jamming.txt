URL: /gps-jamming
Module: GPS Jamming Detector
Category: Military Monitoring
Icon: 📡

DESCRIPTION:
Monitoring of GPS signal loss over regions.
Early indicator of military operations and EW use.

FUNCTIONALITY:
- Display jamming zones on map
- Color coding by intensity:
  🔴 CRITICAL (full jamming)
  🟡 HIGH (partial jamming)
  🟢 NORMAL (no jamming)
- Timeline by region
- Escalation forecast

DATA SOURCES:
- OpenSky Network (aviation data)
- Anomaly analysis in signals
